Ludo Mood

Every token has feelings. Don't hurt them. (Do.)

Players

Tap a seat to switch it between You, Bot and Off. You need at least 2 players.

How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its base.
  • A 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose the turn.
  • Land on an opponent to send them home crying, and roll again.
  • ⭐ stars and start squares are safe. Nobody gets captured there.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre. Get all 4 tokens home to win.
