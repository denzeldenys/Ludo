How to play
  • Tap the screen or flick upwards to throw the dice.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its base.
  • A 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose the turn.
  • Land on an opponent to knock them back to base, and roll again.
  • ⭐ stars and start squares are safe.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre. Get all 4 home to win.
